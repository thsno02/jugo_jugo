据材料引用，LlamaIndex CEO Jerry Liu 主张 LLM 与个人数据结合的系统可创建一个"second brain"（第二大脑）——"not just searchable but can synthesize and surface connections from your own notes"。[^src-1]

作者对此方向性认同（"directionally right"），但基于多年构建多 agent AI 系统的经验提出务实警告："the gap between 'cool demo' and 'daily driver' is always wider than it looks"。devil is in the implementation details（魔鬼在实现细节中）。[^src-2]

这一观点框定了整篇材料的基调：愿景正确，执行层面仍处早期——"The idea is great. The execution? Still pretty rough."[^src-3]
