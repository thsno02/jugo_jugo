作者报告的意外发现：使用 wiki 后写作质量提升形成正循环 [^src-1]。

**循环机制**：
1. 写博客时 grep wiki 找相关概念
2. 拉 TL;DR 入 Claude context
3. Claude 带引用（来自用户自己历史决策）draft
4. 产出 feel grounded instead of generic

作者明确表述："The 'compounding asset' framing isn't a metaphor — it's a real productivity loop." [^src-1]

关键区分：不是引用外部权威而是引用"my own historical decisions"——这是使写作感觉扎实而非泛泛的关键。

[^card-1]: 与 [ripple-effect-ingest] 相关——涟漪效应是复合循环的输入端
[^card-2]: 与 [tldr-enforcement-context-window] 相关——TL;DR 是循环中"拉入 context"步骤的实现

[^src-1]: `data/raw/webpage/openaitoolshub-six-months/markdown.md` -- "What Surprised Me" P56 -- "I write better with the wiki than I did without it...The 'compounding asset' framing isn't a metaphor — it's a real productivity loop."
