Y Combinator 在其 Spring 2026 Requests for Startups（RFS）中明确提出了 "company brain" 作为缺失原语的需求。[^src-1]

原文引述："If we want every company to run on AI automation, we need a new primitive: a company brain. A system that pulls knowledge out of all these fragmented sources, structures it, keeps it current, and turns it into an executable skills file for AI."[^src-1]

该 RFS 信号表明行业共识正在形成：AI automation 的瓶颈不在模型能力，而在结构化、当前、可执行的企业知识层。Falconer 将自身产品定位为该原语的实现。[^card-1]
