## 源证据

- Paraphrasing 表（Table 11，`defense.tex` 第 1009–1023 行）：
  > "NQ Black-Box 0.97/0.96 → 0.87/0.83 ... HotpotQA Black-Box 0.99/1.0 → 0.93/1.0 ... MS-MARCO Black-Box 0.91/0.89 → 0.79/0.70"。
- Paraphrasing 失败原因（第 1027 行）：
  > "PoisonedRAG could still achieve high ASRs and F1-Score, which means paraphrasing defense cannot effectively defend against PoisonedRAG."。
- PPL 检测失败原因（第 1039–1041 行）：
  > "the false positive rate (FPR) is also very large when the true positive rate (TPR) is very large ... the perplexity values of malicious texts are not statistically higher than those of clean texts ... The sub-text I is generated by GPT-4, which is of high quality."。
- Duplicate filtering 表（Table 12，第 1071–1084 行）：
  > "with defense" 列与 "w.o. defense" 列在所有数据集与黑/白盒下 ASR 完全一致（NQ 0.97 → 0.97 等）。
- Duplicate filtering 失败原因（第 1062 行）：
  > "The reason is that the sub-text I (generated by GPT-4 in our experiment) in each malicious text is different, resulting in diverse malicious texts."。
- Knowledge expansion 失败（第 1089 行）：
  > "PoisonedRAG could still achieve 41% (black-box) and 43% (white-box) ASR on HotpotQA when k=50. Additionally, we find that ASR further increases as N increases ... this defense is less effective when an attacker could inject more malicious texts into the knowledge database."。

## 卡片范围是否成立

这张卡的范围是"PoisonedRAG 论文中 §5 列出的四类防御为什么失败"。每条防御的 ASR 数字和论文给出的失败机理都来自 §5 与 Table 11–12 + 附录 figures，没有引入论文外的防御方案或推断。末尾三条"设计原则"是把四个具体失败模式概括成一句话工程教训，停留在论文证据可支撑的范围内：(1) retriever 鲁棒性 ↔ 防御失败 来源于 paraphrasing 失败原因；(2) 内容质量信号不可靠来源于 PPL 段；(3) top-k 多数票被 N 同步增长打败来源于 Knowledge Expansion 段。
