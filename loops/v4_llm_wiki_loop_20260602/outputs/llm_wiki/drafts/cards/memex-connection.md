LLM Wiki 的理念与 Vannevar Bush 1945 年提出的 **Memex** 有精神上的联系——一种个人化的、策展的知识存储，文档之间有关联路径（associative trails）[^src-1]。

作者指出，Bush 的愿景比后来的万维网更接近 LLM Wiki：私有的、主动策展的，文档之间的连接与文档本身同等重要。Bush 无法解决的部分——谁来做维护——由 LLM 承担[^src-2]。

[^src-2]: `data/raw/gist_raw/karpathy-gist-llm-wiki/text.txt` -- "Why this works" 第3段 -- "Bush's vision was closer to this than to what the web became: private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves. The part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The LLM handles that."
