Generic company brains store context. Cognition treats work as learning signal: sessions become approved skills, skills get freshness and outcome history, and agents retrieve executable guidance instead of a pile of notes.
Read the scienceEvidence
Prompts, files, tool calls, decisions, and outcomes become typed learning events.
Consolidation
Raw traces compress into human-approved skills with steps, checks, and failure modes.
Decay
Recall and freshness are modeled over time, so stale guidance can ask first or refresh.
Teaching
The next agent receives a route: which skill, whose judgment, and how to apply it.
Confirm-first capture
Cognition drafts skills and waits for explicit approval before saving anything to the group.
Person-specific retrieval
Skills keep who taught them and why their judgment worked, so agents can follow the right taste.
Group-code setup
One teammate creates the group. Everyone else joins with the same code and starts sharing memory.
